# Make it yours

Use the **Theme** selector at the top of the Tools sidebar to restyle every DevSnip Pro panel at once:

- **System Default** follows your VS Code theme.
- **Dark**, **Midnight**, **Dracula**, **Monokai**, **Nord** and **Cyberpunk** are dark themes.
- **Light** and **High Contrast** are for bright rooms and maximum legibility.

The change applies immediately and is remembered. Every theme is checked for readable contrast.
