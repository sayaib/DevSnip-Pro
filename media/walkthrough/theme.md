# Make it yours

Use the **Theme** selector at the top of the Tools sidebar, or open **Theme Studio**, to restyle every DevSnip Pro panel at once:

- **VS Code Adaptive** follows your VS Code theme (light, dark or high contrast) and is always free.
- Every other theme is unlocked with points you earn by using DevSnip Pro: **Modern Dark**, **Clean Light**, **AMOLED Black** and **High Contrast** cost 25 points; **GitHub Dark**, **GitHub Light**, **Midnight Blue**, **Ocean Blue**, **Nord**, **Monokai**, **Dracula**, **Cyberpunk Neon**, **Purple Gradient**, **Solarized**, **Ember**, **Synthwave** and **Aurora** cost more.
- **Solarized**, **Synthwave** and **Aurora** also unlock for free at Silver, Gold and Platinum, and **Ember** after a 14-day streak.

Click a locked theme to **preview it for 30 seconds** on every DevSnip Pro panel. A countdown in the status bar lets you unlock it or switch back early, and your own theme returns when time is up. The **Rewards** tab in Milestones & Points has a Preview button for every locked theme too.

**Theme Studio** (`DevSnip Pro: Theme Studio`) adds:

- **Follow VS Code**: use one DevSnip theme while VS Code is dark and another while it is light.
- **Colours**: any accent colour, and your own background for DevSnip themes. Colours are adjusted when needed so text keeps WCAG AA contrast.
- **Shape**: sharp to round corners, subtle or strong borders, flat or elevated shadows.
- **Typography**: interface and code fonts, or keep VS Code's.
- **Motion**: turn the theme fade off. It is always off when your system asks for reduced motion.

Changes apply immediately and are remembered across restarts. Every theme is checked for readable contrast.
