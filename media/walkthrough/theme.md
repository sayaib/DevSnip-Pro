# Make it yours

Use the **Theme** selector at the top of the Tools sidebar to restyle every DevSnip Pro panel at once:

- **System Default** follows your VS Code theme and is always free.
- Every other theme is unlocked with points you earn by using DevSnip Pro: **Dark**, **Light** and **High Contrast** cost 25 points, and **Midnight**, **Nord**, **Monokai**, **Dracula**, **Cyberpunk**, **Solarized**, **Ember**, **Synthwave** and **Aurora** cost more.
- **Solarized**, **Synthwave** and **Aurora** also unlock for free at Silver, Gold and Platinum, and **Ember** after a 14-day streak.

Click a locked theme to **preview it for 30 seconds** on every DevSnip Pro panel. A countdown in the status bar lets you unlock it or switch back early, and your own theme returns when time is up. The **Rewards** tab in Milestones & Points has a Preview button for every locked theme too.

The change applies immediately and is remembered. Every theme is checked for readable contrast.
